Violet Glass is an Android launcher with customizable home screens, a glass theme and app categories. Arrange apps, folders and widgets, choose a grid and dock, and open the app drawer with a swipe.

Add a dedicated panel for your own Home Assistant dashboard. Enable a desktop companion, wallpaper effects or both. The companion moves around icons and widgets and can show an envelope for notifications from selected apps when Android notification access is granted.

Export and restore your layout and preferences. The launcher interface is primarily in Polish.
